Procurement Module — User Guide

Step-by-step guide for using the IncapttaPro Procurement module

📅 Last updated: March 2026 📦 Module: Procurement 🔖 Version: 1.0

Overview

The Procurement module manages the entire process of purchasing goods and services — from raising an initial request, through receiving goods, to paying the supplier. Every step is tracked, approved, and linked together.

6 Sections

Suppliers · Requisitions · Purchase Orders · Goods Receipts · Invoices · Payments

Full Audit Trail

Every action is timestamped and linked back to the original request and the person who acted.

Finance Integration

Payments are recorded directly into the Finance ledger — no double entry needed.

ℹ️
Access the Procurement module from the top navigation bar. Look for the Procurement menu — it contains links to each section.

2. Purchase Requisitions

A Purchase Requisition (PR) is an internal request to purchase goods or services. It must be approved before a Purchase Order can be created and sent to a supplier.

Creating a Requisition

1
Go to Procurement → Requisitions → New Requisition

Or click the "New Requisition" button on the Requisitions list.

2
Fill in the header details

Title, Procurement Type, and Department are required. You can optionally link a Supplier at this stage.

3
Add line items

Click "Add Line Item" for each item you need. Fill in the Item Name, SKU, Unit of Measure, Cost Price, and Markup %.

4
Save as Draft or Submit

"Save as Draft" lets you come back and edit. "Submit" sends it for approval.

4. Goods Receipt Notes (GRN)

A Goods Receipt Note (GRN) records the actual goods received against a Purchase Order. It is the physical check — quantities received are compared to what was ordered, and any differences (variances) are flagged.

⚠️
Only Storekeepers, Managers, Admins, and Super Admins can create GRNs. If you received goods but cannot see the "Receive Goods" button, ask your manager or storekeeper to record it.

Recording a Goods Receipt

1
Open the Purchase Order and click "Receive Goods"

The PO must be in SENT or ACKNOWLEDGED status.

2
Set the Date Received

Defaults to today. Change it if the goods were received on a different day.

3
Enter quantities received for each line item

The "Ordered" column shows the PO quantity. Enter the actual quantity received in the "Received" column. The variance is calculated automatically.

4
Add notes for any issues

Use the item-level notes field to record damage, substitutions, or anything else to note about specific items.

5
Confirm or save as draft

If all quantities match, click "Confirm Receipt". If there are variances, you must tick the acknowledgement checkbox before you can confirm.

Handling Variances

A variance means the quantity received differs from the quantity ordered. The system highlights variances in amber (under-received) or blue (over-received).

Variance TypeWhat it meansWhat to do
Under-receivedFewer items arrived than ordered (negative variance)Tick the acknowledgement and confirm — the outstanding qty remains on the PO
Over-receivedMore items arrived than ordered (positive variance)Confirm with acknowledgement, or mark as Disputed to flag with supplier
DisputedQuality or quantity issues you cannot acceptClick "Mark as Disputed" and enter a reason — this flags the GRN for review

Inventory Posting

Once a GRN is confirmed, the system automatically updates inventory. You will see the posting status on the GRN detail page:

⏳ PENDING

The inventory update is queued. This is normal immediately after confirming.

🔄 PROCESSING

Inventory is being updated. Wait a moment.

✅ COMPLETE

Inventory updated. You can now create the supplier invoice.

❌ FAILED

Something went wrong. Contact your system administrator.

💡
The "Create Invoice" button only appears once inventory posting is COMPLETE. If you do not see it, refresh the page and check the posting status.

5. Supplier Invoices

After goods are received and inventory is updated, you enter the supplier's invoice. The system performs a 3-way match — comparing the invoice to the Purchase Order and the GRN — to detect discrepancies before payment is made.

Creating an Invoice from a GRN

1
Open the confirmed GRN

Go to Procurement → Goods Receipts, find the confirmed GRN, and open it.

2
Click "Create Invoice"

This button appears only when: GRN is CONFIRMED, inventory posting is COMPLETE, and no invoice has been created yet.

3
Enter the supplier's invoice number and date

Match these exactly to the physical invoice you received from the supplier.

4
Check and adjust line item amounts

The system pre-fills amounts from the GRN. Adjust the quantities or unit costs if the supplier's invoice differs.

5
Upload the invoice scan (recommended)

Attach a photo or PDF of the physical invoice for records.

6
Review the match result and submit

The system shows whether the invoice matches the GRN. Submit for approval.

Understanding the Match Result

The system compares what the supplier is charging to what was actually received. The result is one of:

Match StatusWhat it meansWhat happens next
Matched Invoice total equals the expected amount (within rounding) Accountant can approve immediately
Minor Variance Difference is less than 10% — likely rounding or minor price changes Accountant can approve with a note
Variance Exceeded Difference is 10% or more — significant price discrepancy An override reason is required before approval is possible
Override Approved A large variance was manually reviewed and accepted Approval allowed — override reason is saved on record
💡
Expected amount is calculated as: quantity received × unit cost from the PO. If the supplier charges more, there is a variance. Check the physical invoice carefully before entering amounts.

🚩 Flag as Disputed

6. Processing Payments

Once an invoice is APPROVED, an Accountant, Admin, or Super Admin can process the payment. This records the expense in the Finance module and deducts the amount from the selected account balance.

🔒
Managers cannot process payments. This is a system-enforced control. If you are a Manager and need a payment processed, ask your Accountant or Admin.

Recording a Payment

1
Open the approved invoice

Go to Procurement → Invoices, filter by "Approved", and open the invoice.

2
Click "Process Payment"

This button is only visible on APPROVED invoices.

3
Select the payment account

Choose from Bank, EcoCash, Cash on Hand, or IRD. The current balance of each account is shown next to its name.

4
Enter the payment reference

This should be the bank transaction reference, EcoCash transaction ID, or cash voucher number. It is your proof of payment.

5
Set the transaction date

Defaults to today. Change if the payment was made on a different date.

6
Review the Finance Record Preview

The blue preview card shows exactly how the expense will appear in the Finance ledger. Check that everything is correct.

7
Click "Record Payment"

The payment is recorded, the invoice is marked as PAID, and the account balance is updated immediately.

Payment Accounts

AccountUse for
BankBank transfer payments — enter the bank reference number
EcoCashMobile money payments — enter the EcoCash transaction ID (e.g. ECO-20260328-001)

Insufficient balance warning: If the account you selected does not have enough funds, you will see an amber warning. Review your account balances and either top up the account or use a different payment method before proceeding.

After Payment

Once recorded, the invoice detail page shows a Payment Details card with the payment method, reference, and a link to the Finance ledger record. The payment cannot be reversed from this module — contact your Finance administrator if a correction is needed.

Job Card Integration — Overview

The Procurement module is directly embedded inside Job Cards. When a job card item has insufficient stock, staff can raise a Purchase Requisition without leaving the job card. Once raised, the entire procurement pipeline status is visible inline — from PR through to payment — without navigating to the Procurement module at all.

Stock Check

Every job card inventory line shows a live stock badge — green if stock is sufficient, amber if stock is low, red if insufficient.

Raise PR in One Click

When stock is insufficient, an amber "Raise PR" button appears. Click it to open the PR panel and submit a requisition pre-filled with the job card item details.

Live Pipeline Status

After a PR is raised, the button is replaced by a live procurement pipeline tracker showing the current stage: PR → PO → GRN → Invoice → Paid.

💡
No double-entry. The job card item is automatically linked to the PR, PO, GRN, and Invoice as they are created. The pipeline status updates in real time — you never need to manually link records.

Stock Badges on Job Card Items

On the Job Card detail page, each inventory line item displays a stock badge in the Procurement column showing whether there is enough stock to fulfil the job card requirement.

BadgeMeaningWhat to do
✓ In Stock Current on-hand stock meets or exceeds the job requirement No action needed — stock will be allocated when the job is dispatched
⚠ Low Stock Some stock available but not enough to fully meet the requirement Consider raising a PR for the shortfall amount
✗ No Stock Zero stock — the item is not available at all Raise a PR immediately to procure the item
ℹ️
The stock count shown is a snapshot taken when the item was added to the job card. The badge reflects the current live stock from the inventory system, which may have changed since then.

Raising a PR from a Job Card

When a job card item does not have enough stock, an amber "Raise PR" button appears next to the stock badge. This is only visible to Managers, Admins, and Super Admins.

⚠️
The "Raise PR" button only appears when: (1) the item has an inventory item selected, (2) on-hand stock is less than the qty required, and (3) no PR has already been raised for this item.

1

End-to-End Example

Here is a complete walkthrough of how a job card item moves from insufficient stock all the way through to supplier payment.

1
Job card item shows insufficient stock

A job card for a poultry structure installation requires 50 roof sheets. The stock badge shows ✗ No Stock — only 12 are on hand. The "Raise PR" button appears.

2
Manager raises a PR from the job card

The manager clicks "Raise PR". The panel slides in pre-filled with 38 sheets (the shortfall), the unit cost, and the job card reference. They select the preferred supplier and click Submit.

3
Pipeline card appears — stage: Requisition

The button is replaced by: PR-2026-0042 — Requisition. The PR is SUBMITTED and awaiting approval.

4
Admin approves the PR and creates a PO

In Procurement → Requisitions, the admin approves PR-2026-0042 and clicks "Create Purchase Order". The pipeline card on the job card automatically updates to show PO as the current stage.

5
PO sent to supplier, goods arrive — GRN created

The storekeeper clicks "Receive Goods" on the PO, records 38 sheets received, and confirms the GRN. Inventory is updated automatically. Pipeline stage advances to GRN.

6
Accountant creates and approves the supplier invoice

From the confirmed GRN, the accountant clicks "Create Invoice", enters the supplier's invoice number and amounts, checks the 3-way match result (Matched ✓), and submits for approval. They then approve it. Pipeline shows Invoice stage active.

7
Payment processed — pipeline shows Paid

The accountant clicks "Process Payment", selects Bank, enters the transaction reference, and records the payment. The pipeline card on the job card now shows all five steps complete with a green Paid stage. The job card item's procurement is fully resolved.

💡
At any point the job card manager can expand the pipeline card and click the document links (PR number, PO number, GRN number, Invoice number) to jump directly to those records and check details, add notes, or take action — without losing their place in the job card.

3-Way Match Explained

The 3-way match is an automatic check that compares three documents before allowing payment:

Document 1: Purchase Order

What you agreed to buy, at what price, from which supplier.

Document 2: GRN

What you actually received — the quantities recorded when goods arrived.

Document 3: Supplier Invoice

What the supplier is charging you.

The system calculates the expected amount from the GRN (qty received × unit cost), then compares it to the invoice amount. The difference is the variance.

Variance Thresholds

VarianceResultAction Required
0%MatchedNone — approve and pay
0.1% – 9.99%Minor VarianceAccountant can approve with acknowledgement
10% or moreVariance ExceededOverride reason required before approval
💡
A variance exceeding 10% is a red flag. Common causes include: supplier changed unit prices without notice, quantity discrepancy between GRN and invoice, additional charges (freight, handling) added to the invoice. Always investigate before providing an override reason.

Common Questions

I confirmed the GRN but the "Create Invoice" button does not appear

Wait a moment and refresh the page. The inventory posting happens automatically but can take 30–60 seconds. The "Create Invoice" button only appears once the posting status shows COMPLETE.

If it shows FAILED after several minutes, contact your system administrator.

The invoice I need to enter has different prices than the PO

Enter the supplier's actual invoice amounts in the line items on the Create Invoice form. The system will calculate the variance and flag it. If the variance is within 10%, the accountant can approve with a note. If over 10%, a written override reason is required. Do not change amounts to hide a real discrepancy — the variance is recorded for audit purposes.

Can I create a GRN if only part of the order arrived?

Yes. Enter the quantities actually received (even if less than ordered). The PO status will move to IN PROGRESS. You can create another GRN when the remaining items arrive.

I made a mistake on the GRN — can I edit it?

You can edit a GRN while it is in DRAFT status. Once it is CONFIRMED, it cannot be edited. If there is a confirmed error, mark the GRN as DISPUTED to flag it, and contact your manager.

The wrong payment account was selected — can it be reversed?

Payment records cannot be reversed from the Procurement module once recorded. Contact your Finance administrator or system admin for a correction in the Finance ledger.

Why can I not see the "Approve Invoice" button?

The Approve Invoice button is only visible to Accountants, Admins, and Super Admins. Managers cannot approve invoices — this is a system control. Also, invoices with a variance exceeding 10% cannot be approved until an override reason has been entered.

Can I create a PO directly without a Requisition?

No. All Purchase Orders must trace back to an approved Purchase Requisition. This ensures all purchases are authorised before being placed with a supplier.

What is the due date on an invoice?

The due date is automatically suggested based on the supplier's payment terms (e.g. NET30 = 30 days from invoice date). You can change it if needed. Overdue invoices are highlighted in red on the invoice list.
